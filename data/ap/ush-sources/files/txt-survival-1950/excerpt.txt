The secrets of survival are: KNOW THE BOMB’S TRUE DANGERS. KNOW THE STEPS YOU CAN TAKE TO ESCAPE THEM. [...] To begin with, you must realize that atom-splitting is just another way of causing an explosion. While an atom bomb holds more death and destruction than man has ever before wrapped in a single package, its total power is definitely limited. Not even hydrogen bombs could blow the earth apart or kill us all by mysterious radiation. Because the power of all bombs is limited, your chances of living through an atomic attack are much better than you may have thought.
