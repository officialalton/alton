# ALTON Admissions Consultant Independent Contractor Services Agreement

Agreement Version 0.1-EN

## 1. Parties and Location

This Agreement is between Alton Education LLC (the “Company”) and the consultant identified in Schedule A (the “Consultant”) for services performed in the United States, including California, or in another country identified in Schedule A. The Consultant must disclose the actual country, state or province, and city where services are performed and obtain written acknowledgment of any location change before performing services there. The compensation currency is stated in Schedule A; United States services are denominated in USD and Korean services in KRW unless a lawful written agreement expressly specifies otherwise.

The parties engage on an independent contractor basis to the extent permitted by applicable law. The Consultant determines the methods and organization of accepted services subject to agreed deliverables, appointments, and safety and privacy requirements. The Consultant may accept or decline future assignments and serve other clients. The Consultant has no authority to bind the Company without written authorization. The title of this Agreement does not determine legal classification. Applicable mandatory employment, tax, social insurance, privacy, and other protections remain effective. If applicable law treats the relationship as employment, all mandatory employment rights and Company obligations apply notwithstanding the parties' description of the relationship.

## 2. Accepted Consulting Services

Services include admissions guidance, educational and career roadmaps, application planning, student progress review, and parent communications within the accepted monthly scope. The Consultant may accept or decline future engagements, serves other clients, and controls the professional methods used to deliver agreed work. Meetings and milestones are arranged by agreement. No admission, scholarship, score, or other educational outcome is guaranteed. The Consultant must not impersonate a student, fabricate credentials, write a student's application as the student's own work, or promise admissions influence.

The monthly scope and deliverables are recorded in Schedule A or a mutually accepted written assignment. The monthly fee covers that agreed scope, including related preparation, review, documentation, and required meetings. No unilateral expansion of the monthly scope is permitted. Work outside it requires separately agreed written compensation. The Consultant must not appoint a substitute or disclose student information to another provider without written authorization and required safeguards.

## 3. Monthly Fee and Partial Periods

The fee is the accepted monthly amount and currency stated under “Monthly fee:” in Schedule A. United States engagements use USD and Korean engagements use KRW unless a lawful written agreement specifies otherwise. The fee is not an hourly wage, commission, success fee, or admissions-contingent payment.

For a full calendar month, the fee is allocated by calendar day across that month. The 1st–15th portion is the monthly fee multiplied by 15 divided by the number of days in that month; the remaining portion is the balance. A starting or ending partial month is prorated by calendar days in the agreed service term divided by the number of days in the month. Rounding follows the payment currency's minor unit, with any remainder reconciled in the final payment for the month. Days within an ongoing monthly engagement are not removed merely because no meeting occurs that day. Additional agreed fees are itemized separately. Disputes over deliverables do not automatically forfeit earned fees; mandatory payment rights prevail.

## 4. Scheduling and Professional Responsibilities

The Consultant promptly reports inability to attend an accepted meeting and arranges a reasonable replacement meeting with the client and Company. Student absence does not automatically cancel the monthly fee. Any agreed reduction or change in services is documented without retroactively changing completed work. The Consultant provides accurate guidance, identifies limits of their expertise, and refers regulated legal, immigration, tax, or financial questions to appropriately qualified professionals.

## 5. Statements and Payment

Settlement is semimonthly, with pay periods and payment dates measured in Pacific Time (America/Los_Angeles). Services performed from the 1st through the 15th of a month are received in the designated recipient account no later than the 26th of the same month; services performed from the 16th through the last day of a month are received in the designated recipient account no later than the 10th of the following month. The Company provides a statement for each pay period on or before the payment date. If a payday falls on a weekend or U.S. federal bank holiday, payment is due on the preceding business day. ALTON initiates the transfer so that payment is received in the recipient's account by the deadline; delays caused by the receiving bank or incorrect account details provided by the recipient are not a breach. The Consultant should report a discrepancy within five business days after receiving the statement; missing that period does not forfeit a valid payment claim.

The statement identifies the monthly fee and currency, service period, prorated amounts where applicable, separately agreed fees, and lawful adjustments. ALTON bears its own transfer and currency-conversion fees for payments to the Consultant; fees charged by the recipient's bank are not covered. The payment method is stated in Schedule A. No undisclosed fee or student refund is automatically deducted from earned compensation. Disputed amounts are identified and discussed promptly; undisputed amounts remain payable on schedule.

## 6. Tax and Business Responsibilities

The Consultant is responsible for the taxes that apply to the Consultant. The Company complies with its own statutory withholding and reporting obligations where applicable. Tax forms and related documentation are handled outside this Agreement.

Subject to mandatory law and the agreed arrangement, the Consultant is responsible for their own business registrations, taxes, insurance, and ordinary equipment and connectivity costs. Any agreed reimbursable expense requires appropriate documentation. These terms do not transfer obligations that the Company must bear by law.

## 7. Safety and Communications

The Consultant uses approved student communication and meeting channels, maintains professional boundaries, and promptly reports safeguarding, privacy, or security concerns. Applicable reporting duties remain effective. Unauthorized recordings, credential sharing, inappropriate private contact, and disclosure of student information are prohibited. Qualification checks and any legally required screening authorizations are handled through the applicable onboarding process.

## 8. Video Recording, Audio Recording, Transcription, and AI Lesson Notes

This section covers four items for regular lessons and follow-up consultations: video recording and retention of the recordings, audio recording and retention of the recordings, conversion of speech into a text transcript and retention of the transcript, and AI-assisted preparation and storage of lesson notes and summaries (Smart Notes) and retention of those notes and summaries. Currently provided: transcripts and Smart Notes. Video recording and audio recording are not currently provided; this section applies to them if and when ALTON offers them, and ALTON confirms the applicable consents before any recording is activated. Covered content may include participant images, voices, names, spoken questions and answers, shared lesson screens, instructional materials, and meeting metadata. These processes support instruction, review, preparation, quality and safety management, and teacher handover.

Consent coverage is separate from execution. Trial lessons are always excluded from recording, transcription and AI meeting notes, including after a contract is signed. A first consultation is excluded from video and audio recording and from retained transcripts; AI meeting notes of a first consultation are prepared only where the requester consented when requesting the consultation, are visible only to ALTON staff, and are eligible for deletion one year after the consultation ends. Recording, transcription, and AI notes for regular lessons and follow-up consultations apply only after the applicable agreement and all required participant consents are complete. Signing or accepting this section does not itself start any recording, transcription, or note-taking. The signing participant expressly consents to the four items above for authorized regular lessons and follow-up consultations. A parent or legal guardian authorizes processing of the named child's participation within their lawful authority. ALTON provides notice before recording and obtains consent from all participants as required by applicable law. Additional attendees must receive notice and give required consent before capture begins. A parent signature does not substitute for another participant's consent. Recording must not start until the applicable agreement, required parental verification, and participant consent are complete.

ALTON uses designated Google Workspace, Meet, Drive, and Gemini services and authorized providers acting for the stated purposes. Recordings, transcripts, and AI notes are stored with restricted access. Authorized teachers and staff may access them for their duties. Students and verified guardians may view lesson artifacts made available to their accounts; this does not grant unrestricted download, redistribution, or access to another family's records or private student notes and whiteboards. No public posting, unrelated advertising, sale, or unrestricted model training is authorized by this consent.

Video and audio recordings (if offered), transcripts, and AI notes and summaries become eligible for deletion one year after the end date of each lesson or consultation, regardless of continued enrolment or re-enrolment (the full retention schedule appears in the Privacy Policy), and lesson materials are kept for 1 year after the last lesson. Preservation exceptions are limited to what is necessary for a legal requirement, an active dispute or legal hold, or an accounting or safety need, and are reviewed at least every 12 months. Children's information is deleted earlier when its purpose is achieved or a parent requests deletion, except where a preservation exception applies. Other records are kept only as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, the student's service relationship, and applicable legal obligations or claims. Records no longer needed are securely deleted, subject to lawful preservation requirements. Parents and participants may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent at official@alton.education. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not make prior authorized processing unlawful. ALTON stops affected processing as required and resolves future lessons and unused paid credits under the applicable agreement without imposing a penalty for exercising statutory rights. Free learning access is not conditioned on agreeing to lesson recording.

AI notes and transcripts may contain omissions, speaker errors, or inaccuracies. Corrections may be requested. AI output alone does not conclusively determine attendance, wages, fees, refunds, or disputes. Material changes receive updated notice and renewed consent when required.

Required review, correction, and documentation within the accepted monthly scope are covered by the monthly fee; additional work requires written compensation under Sections 2 and 3.

## 9. Confidentiality and Data Security

Use nonpublic student, family, security, and business information only for accepted services. Apply reasonable security, restrict access, report incidents promptly, and return or securely remove information when access ends, subject to lawful retention. Company records are retained under the retention schedule in ALTON's Privacy Policy. Follow applicable cross-border transfer and data-processing requirements. The Consultant may not use student data for unrelated marketing or solicitations.

Nothing prohibits protected reporting, cooperation with authorities, legal advice, or other nonwaivable rights. Qualifying confidential trade-secret disclosures protected by applicable law remain protected.

## 10. Materials and Intellectual Property

The Consultant retains pre-existing materials identified in Schedule B. Upon payment, the Consultant assigns to the Company, to the extent legally transferable, rights in new materials expressly commissioned for Company ownership under an accepted assignment. Ordinary teaching does not transfer unrelated pre-existing works.

For authorized incorporation of prior materials, Schedule B identifies the license required for the agreed service use. Third-party materials require lawful permission. Student work and personal information are not transferred as unrestricted Company property. Mandatory author rights and nonassignable rights remain protected.

## 11. Other Clients and Conflicts

The Consultant may serve other clients. The Consultant must not misuse confidential information, falsely represent Company authorization, divert an accepted Company engagement through unauthorized use of student contacts, or interfere with agreed services. This Agreement imposes no general post-termination noncompetition restriction.

## 12. Duration and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date in Schedule A. Either party may terminate upon 30 days' written notice. The notice period does not permit withholding or delaying final earned fees beyond the applicable payment deadline. The parties may end an affected assignment promptly when necessary for safety, material breach, or a legal requirement, subject to applicable law and payment for earned services.

Termination does not forfeit earned fees. The parties resolve outstanding statements, return property, and arrange a reasonable student handover. Continuing confidentiality and valid intellectual-property obligations survive only to the extent lawful.

## 13. Responsibility and Law

Each party is responsible for its own breaches and conduct under applicable law. Nothing excludes liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Events beyond reasonable control require prompt notice and reasonable mitigation; they do not automatically cancel payment for services already earned.

California law and applicable federal law govern subject to mandatory protections in the Consultant's actual work jurisdiction. The parties seek good-faith resolution without preventing urgent relief, agency reporting, or timely claims. Disputes may be brought before a court with lawful jurisdiction, subject to mandatory venue rules. This Agreement does not require arbitration or waive nonwaivable rights.

## 13.1 California and Other Mandatory Local Protections

For services performed in California, nothing in this Agreement waives mandatory worker classification, payment, expense reimbursement, leave, safety, or other protections that apply to the actual relationship. A tax form, account designation, or this Agreement does not alone determine status. Any required employment protections prevail over inconsistent terms, including the termination notice period. California invention-assignment exclusions and protected reporting rights apply where legally required. For services performed in Korea or another jurisdiction, applicable mandatory worker, tax, social insurance, privacy, and author-right protections likewise prevail.

## 14. Entire Agreement and Execution

This Agreement, completed schedules, and accepted written assignments govern the services. Material changes require written acceptance. Platform settings cannot retroactively alter accepted fees. An unenforceable term is severed only to the extent lawful. Both parties receive a copy of the executed Agreement. The Consultant signs electronically and the Company accepts through its authenticated authorized-representative electronic approval recorded in the executed Agreement. Each party receives a retrievable copy. Company approval does not dispense with the Consultant's signature.
