# ALTON Teacher Hourly Rate Change Addendum

Agreement Version 0.2-EN

## 1. Parties and Existing Agreement

This Addendum is between Alton Education LLC (the “Company”) and the Teacher identified in Schedule A. It amends only the compensation provisions of the executed services agreement identified by its agreement ID and signature date in Schedule A (the “Existing Agreement”). Both the Existing Agreement and this Addendum remain part of the parties' contract records.

## 2. Prospective Rate Change

The previous accepted hourly rate and currency are replaced by the new accepted hourly rate and currency in Schedule A beginning on the effective date stated there. Lesson compensation remains calculated by the minute from recognized lesson time. Required preparation, assignment review, meeting-notes review, and required meetings and training are paid at the same applicable hourly rate from recorded actual minutes. Other additional services require separate written compensation.

Lessons starting before the effective date retain the previous rate, including any recognized cancellation or no-show compensation for those bookings. Lessons starting on or after that date use the new rate. Required nonlesson work uses the rate applicable when the work is performed; work spanning the effective date is recorded separately. Currency conversion must not be used to obscure a rate change. The agreed currency for each period is preserved in the statement.

## 3. Acceptance and Unchanged Terms

This change is effective only when the Teacher has signed and the Company's authenticated authorized-representative approval is recorded. The effective date cannot precede completion of both acceptances. A system profile update alone does not amend the signed rate, and earned compensation is not retroactively reduced.

All other terms of the Existing Agreement remain unchanged, including payment deadlines, Company-paid transfer and conversion fees, and applicable mandatory protections. This Addendum does not change payment dates; any agreement to change those dates must separately identify the old and new schedule, effective date, and required acceptance, and must not override a legal payment deadline. The parties receive a retrievable copy and preserve the original signed documents.
