# ALTON Teacher Services Agreement — Services Outside the United States

Agreement Version 0.2-EN | October 6, 2026

## 1. Parties and Location

This Agreement is between Alton Education LLC (the “Company”) and the teacher identified in Schedule A (the “Teacher”) for services performed entirely outside the United States. The Teacher must disclose their actual work country and notify the Company before changing it or performing services in the United States.

The parties intend an independent service relationship where legally permissible. The title of this Agreement does not determine legal classification. Applicable mandatory employment, tax, social insurance, privacy, and other protections remain effective. If the proposed arrangement requires employment or another structure, the parties must establish that structure before continuing affected services.

## 2. Accepted Services

The Teacher may accept or decline offered assignments. An accepted assignment identifies subjects, lesson length, schedule, and agreed deliverables. No minimum assignments or income are guaranteed. Subject to agreed deliverables, student safety, privacy, and service standards, the Teacher controls their teaching methods and organization of work.

The Teacher may not substitute another person or disclose student information to a substitute without written authorization and completion of required safeguards. Additional services beyond the accepted scope require an agreed written fee or applicable hourly basis before performance. No required work is presumed unpaid merely because it occurs outside a lesson.

## 3. Fees and Recognized Lesson Time

The lesson-service fee is **USD $50.00 per 60 minutes of recognized lesson time**, calculated by the minute. A recognized 120-minute lesson pays $100.00; a recognized 60-minute trial pays $50.00. The accepted rate is recorded for each assignment. A later rate change applies prospectively only after written agreement.

Recognized lesson time is determined from the accepted booking and documented completion or cancellation decision, rather than meeting connection logs alone. Required nonlesson work, including preparation, review, documentation, meetings, or training, must have its scope and compensation recorded in Schedule A or an accepted written assignment. Mandatory payment rights under applicable law prevail.

## 4. Cancellation and Attendance

If the Teacher cancels a lesson, no lesson-service fee is earned for that cancelled lesson, without reducing payment for separate work already performed. If the Teacher is late by 10 minutes or more, undelivered time is documented and made up; the completed services are included in settlement without double payment for the same time.

For an accepted regular 120-minute lesson, a student cancellation less than 24 hours before the scheduled start or a confirmed student no-show is recognized as 120 minutes when the Teacher was ready and available to provide the lesson. Student lateness does not shorten recognized time when the Teacher remains available for the scheduled period. A no-show requires the Company's documented confirmation process. Other cancellations and technical interruptions are settled according to the accepted assignment and actual services delivered, subject to applicable law.

## 5. Statements and Payment

Settlement is semimonthly, with pay periods and payment dates measured in Pacific Time (America/Los_Angeles). Services performed from the 1st through the 15th of a month are paid no later than the 26th of the same month; services performed from the 16th through the last day of a month are paid no later than the 10th of the following month. The Company provides a statement for each pay period on or before the payment date. If a payday falls on a weekend or U.S. federal bank holiday, payment is made on the preceding business day. The Teacher should report a discrepancy within five business days after receiving the statement; missing that period does not forfeit a valid payment claim.

The statement identifies assignments, recognized minutes, applicable rates, separately payable services, and lawful adjustments. The Company bears all bank, transfer, intermediary, and currency-conversion fees for payments to the Teacher, so the Teacher receives the full statement amount. The payment method is stated in Schedule A. No undisclosed fee or student refund is automatically deducted from earned compensation. Disputed amounts are identified and discussed promptly; undisputed amounts remain payable on schedule.

## 6. Tax and Business Responsibilities

The Teacher provides accurate tax-status documentation applicable to their circumstances, including Form W-8BEN when appropriate, and notifies the Company of changes. The Company makes any legally required withholding and reporting and provides supporting records. Neither an IRS form nor foreign citizenship alone determines worker classification.

Subject to mandatory law and the agreed arrangement, the Teacher is responsible for their own business registrations, taxes, insurance, and ordinary equipment and connectivity costs. Any agreed reimbursable expense requires appropriate documentation. These terms do not transfer obligations that the Company must bear by law.

## 7. Safety and Communications

The Teacher uses approved student communication and meeting channels, maintains professional boundaries, and promptly reports safeguarding, privacy, or security concerns. Applicable reporting duties remain effective. Unauthorized recordings, credential sharing, inappropriate private contact, and disclosure of student information are prohibited. Qualification checks and any legally required screening authorizations are handled through the applicable onboarding process.

## 8. Video Recording, Audio Recording, Transcription, and AI Lesson Notes

Regular paid lessons include video recording, audio recording, conversion of speech into a text transcript, storage of recordings and transcripts, and AI-assisted preparation and storage of lesson notes and summaries. Recorded content may include participant images, voices, names, spoken questions and answers, shared lesson screens, instructional materials, and meeting metadata. These processes support instruction, review, preparation, quality and safety management, and teacher handover. They are required components of regular lessons; initial consultations and trial lessons are excluded.

The signing participant expressly consents to these activities for authorized regular lessons. A parent or legal guardian authorizes processing of the named child's participation within their lawful authority. ALTON provides notice before recording and obtains consent from all participants as required by applicable law. Additional attendees must receive notice and give required consent before capture begins. A parent signature does not substitute for another participant's consent. Recording must not start until the applicable agreement, required parental verification, and participant consent are complete.

ALTON uses designated Google Workspace, Meet, Drive, and Gemini services and authorized providers acting for the stated purposes. Recordings, transcripts, and AI notes are stored with restricted access. Authorized teachers and staff may access them for their duties. Students and verified guardians may view lesson artifacts made available to their accounts; this does not grant unrestricted download, redistribution, or access to another family's records or private student notes and whiteboards. No public posting, unrelated advertising, sale, or unrestricted model training is authorized by this consent.

Recordings, transcripts, AI notes, and lesson materials are kept for 1 year after the last lesson (the full retention schedule appears in the Privacy Policy), and longer only where required by law, an active dispute or legal hold, or an accounting or safety need. Other records are kept only as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, the student's service relationship, and applicable legal obligations or claims. Records no longer needed are securely deleted, subject to lawful preservation requirements. Parents and participants may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent at official@alton.education. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not make prior authorized processing unlawful. ALTON stops affected processing as required and resolves future lessons and unused paid credits under the applicable agreement without imposing a penalty for exercising statutory rights. Free learning access is not conditioned on agreeing to lesson recording.

AI notes and transcripts may contain omissions, speaker errors, or inaccuracies. Corrections may be requested. AI output alone does not conclusively determine attendance, wages, fees, refunds, or disputes. Material changes receive updated notice and renewed consent when required.

Required review, correction, and documentation must have expressly agreed compensation under Section 3; required work is not presumed unpaid.

## 9. Confidentiality and Data Security

Use nonpublic student, family, security, and business information only for accepted services. Apply reasonable security, restrict access, report incidents promptly, and return or securely remove information when access ends, subject to lawful retention. Company records are retained under the retention schedule in ALTON's Privacy Policy. Follow applicable cross-border transfer and data-processing requirements. The Teacher may not use student data for unrelated marketing or solicitations.

Nothing prohibits protected reporting, cooperation with authorities, legal advice, or other nonwaivable rights. Qualifying confidential trade-secret disclosures protected by applicable law remain protected.

## 10. Materials and Intellectual Property

The Teacher retains pre-existing materials identified in Schedule B. Upon payment, the Teacher assigns to the Company, to the extent legally transferable, rights in new materials expressly commissioned for Company ownership under an accepted assignment. Ordinary teaching does not transfer unrelated pre-existing works.

For authorized incorporation of prior materials, Schedule B identifies the license required for the agreed service use. Third-party materials require lawful permission. Student work and personal information are not transferred as unrestricted Company property. Mandatory author rights and nonassignable rights remain protected.

## 11. Other Clients and Conflicts

The Teacher may serve other clients. The Teacher must not misuse confidential information, falsely represent Company authorization, divert an accepted Company engagement through unauthorized use of student contacts, or interfere with agreed services. This Agreement imposes no general post-termination noncompetition restriction.

## 12. Duration and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date in Schedule A. Either party may terminate on the written notice period completed in Schedule A. The parties may end an affected assignment promptly when necessary for safety, material breach, or a legal requirement, subject to applicable law and payment for earned services.

Termination does not forfeit earned fees. The parties resolve outstanding statements, return property, and arrange a reasonable student handover. Continuing confidentiality and valid intellectual-property obligations survive only to the extent lawful.

## 13. Responsibility and Law

Each party is responsible for its own breaches and conduct under applicable law. Nothing excludes liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Events beyond reasonable control require prompt notice and reasonable mitigation; they do not automatically cancel payment for services already earned.

California law and applicable federal law govern subject to mandatory protections in the Teacher's actual work jurisdiction. The parties seek good-faith resolution without preventing urgent relief, agency reporting, or timely claims. Disputes may be brought before a court with lawful jurisdiction, subject to mandatory venue rules. This Agreement does not require arbitration or waive nonwaivable rights.

## 14. Entire Agreement and Execution

This Agreement, completed schedules, and accepted written assignments govern the services. Material changes require written acceptance. Platform settings cannot retroactively alter accepted fees. An unenforceable term is severed only to the extent lawful. Both parties receive a copy of the executed Agreement. The parties may sign electronically in accordance with applicable law.
