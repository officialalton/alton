## 2. Free Learning and Paid Services

Free practice tests, learning tools, and approved study materials are governed by ALTON's website Terms at https://alton.education/terms and Privacy Notice at https://alton.education/privacy. Free registration, a parent invitation, or a consultation request does not create a paid tutoring purchase.

## 3. Order Terms and Agreement Duration

Each order identifies the product, subjects or services included, number and length of lessons, price, discount, currency, applicable taxes, total payment, credit validity, and refund-policy version. This Agreement has no fixed subscription term, automatic renewal, or obligation to make additional purchases. It remains applicable while services are used. Additional credits are separate voluntary purchases. A material change requires appropriate notice and acceptance before it applies.

## 4. Services and Educational Outcomes

Services may include individual instruction, educational guidance, assignments, materials, and lesson reviews. The current standard individual lesson is 120 minutes unless the order expressly states otherwise. Consulting deliverables, if any, must be identified in the service description or order; not every purchase includes every consulting service.

Subjects, teachers, learning plans, materials, and available schedules may change for educational or operational reasons. ## 5. Teacher Assignment and Changes

Teachers are assigned by Student and subject. A particular teacher is not guaranteed indefinitely. The Customer may request a change, and ALTON may reassign a teacher based on fit, availability, quality, or operational needs.

A change in one subject does not automatically end other services. Historical lessons and reviews remain attributed to the teacher who provided them. Necessary learning context may be shared with the replacement teacher. Future reservations do not automatically transfer; affected reservations and rescheduling instructions will be communicated.

## 6. Lesson Credits

A lesson credit entitles the Student to the lesson described in the order. The standard credit covers one 120-minute lesson. Booking holds the required credit; completion consumes it.

Lesson credits have a 12-month validity period from purchase unless a different period is disclosed before purchase. The lesson must start within that validity period. ALTON generally uses the earliest-expiring credits first and sends reminders 30 and 7 days before expiry. Any expiry restriction is subject to applicable law. Any nonwaivable restriction on expiration prevails.

## 7. Student Cancellation, Lateness, and Absence

A cancellation at least 24 hours before the scheduled start releases the held credit without extending its original expiry. A cancellation less than 24 hours before the start consumes one credit.

If the Student has not joined within 15 minutes after the start, the teacher may report a no-show. A credit is consumed only after the applicable confirmation process. Late cancellations and confirmed no-shows count as consumed lessons for the refund calculation.

A late arrival does not extend the scheduled ending time. ALTON may consider documented illness or exceptional circumstances without promising an exception in every case. Decisions will be recorded.

## 8. Company or Teacher Cancellation and Technical Failure

If ALTON or the teacher cancels, the held credit is released. If its remaining validity is less than 30 days, the restored credit is extended to 30 days after cancellation, subject to applicable law.

For teacher lateness of 10 minutes or more, undelivered time is recorded as make-up time. It may be provided that day by agreement and if scheduling permits, or attached to a later lesson with that teacher. Make-up time is not a separate credit.

If ALTON's platform or designated meeting service prevents a lesson from starting, the credit is not consumed. If a lesson is interrupted, undelivered time is recorded for make-up. Contact ALTON promptly about access problems so the cause and appropriate remedy can be assessed.

## 9. Payment and Price Changes

There is no automatic renewal or automatic additional purchase under this Agreement. Prices are shown before checkout, ordinarily in USD. ALTON bears payment-processing fees; legally required taxes and the final total are disclosed before payment.

Prices and package configurations are not fixed by this Agreement. The order must disclose the actual standalone lesson price used in any package refund calculation. A new price applies to new purchases only, with at least 30 days' advance notice under this Agreement. Existing purchases retain their recorded price and refund basis. Quotes remain valid for their stated period.

## 10. Stopping Services and Refunds

The Customer may stop purchasing or request that services in one or all subjects stop. No separate termination penalty applies. Future reservations must first be cancelled or resolved under Section 7.

Refundable credits are paid, unused credits not held for an unresolved booking. Free or promotional credits have no cash refund value, without affecting statutory rights.

If a refund is requested within seven days after purchase and no paid lesson has started and no credit from that purchase has been consumed, the actual amount paid is refunded in full.

Otherwise the refund is calculated as follows:

**Refund = actual amount paid for that purchase − (consumed lessons × the actual undiscounted standalone lesson price disclosed at purchase).**

The result cannot be less than zero. No additional debt is created by a negative calculation. Late cancellations and confirmed no-shows count as consumed lessons. Remaining credits from a refunded purchase are retired.

If ALTON ends a subject or service because it cannot provide it, unused paid credits are refunded at the actual per-credit purchase price without recapturing the package discount. Other termination circumstances are assessed under applicable law and the facts; this Agreement does not impose automatic forfeiture of all prepaid value.

Approved refunds are submitted to the payment processor within five business days, in the original currency and ordinarily to the original payment method. Posting time depends on the processor. Mandatory refund rights prevail over this policy.

## 11. Account Status and Records

Stopping tutoring does not automatically delete the account or learning history. Free learning may remain available while the account is otherwise eligible. Past credits, expired credits, or ended teacher assignments do not automatically reactivate when the Student returns.

Retention schedule. ALTON keeps records for these periods. Preservation exceptions are limited to what is necessary for a legal requirement, an active dispute or legal hold, or an accounting or safety need, and are reviewed at least every 12 months:

- Contracts, pricing, and consent records; payments, refunds, and the lesson-credit ledger; and teacher and consultant payout and pay records: 7 years after the contract or transaction ends.
- Attendance, bookings, lesson-credit history, and learning history (homework, note results, reviews, confirmed attendance, and quality-review outcomes): 3 years after the last lesson. Free-member learning records: 3 years after last activity.
- Lesson transcripts and AI lesson notes (Smart Notes): eligible for deletion 1 year after each lesson, regardless of continued enrolment; lesson materials: 1 year after the last lesson. Lesson video and audio recordings are not currently provided; if offered in the future, they are kept for the same period. Children's information is deleted earlier when its purpose is achieved or a parent requests deletion, except where a preservation exception applies.
- Chat and consultation records: 2 years after the matter ends.
- Security and access audit logs: 1 year after creation. Notifications: 90 days.
- Account closure: a 30-day period in which a closure request can be cancelled; deleted data in backups is removed within 35 days. A closed account keeps only what a retention basis above requires, with restricted access.

Account closure and privacy requests are separate processes. A standard closure workflow may include a 30-day cancellation window, but that workflow does not postpone legally required privacy-request deadlines. Identity verification, lawful record retention, and applicable deletion exceptions may apply.

## 12. Video Recording, Audio Recording, Transcription, and AI Lesson Notes

Regular paid lessons include conversion of speech into a text transcript, storage of transcripts, and AI-assisted preparation and storage of lesson notes and summaries (Smart Notes). Lesson video recording and audio recording, and storage of recordings, are not currently provided; if ALTON offers them in the future, this clause also covers them, and ALTON confirms the applicable consents before any recording is activated. Lesson content may include participant images, voices, names, spoken questions and answers, shared lesson screens, instructional materials, and meeting metadata. These processes support instruction, review, preparation, quality and safety management, and teacher handover. They are required components of regular lessons; initial consultations and trial lessons are excluded.

The signing participant expressly consents to these activities for authorized regular lessons. A parent or legal guardian authorizes processing of the named child's participation within their lawful authority. ALTON provides notice before recording and obtains consent from all participants as required by applicable law. Additional attendees must receive notice and give required consent before capture begins. A parent signature does not substitute for another participant's consent. Recording must not start until the applicable agreement, required parental verification, and participant consent are complete.

ALTON uses designated Google Workspace, Meet, Drive, and Gemini services and authorized providers acting for the stated purposes. Recordings, transcripts, and AI notes are stored with restricted access. Authorized teachers and staff may access them for their duties. Students and verified guardians may view lesson artifacts made available to their accounts; this does not grant unrestricted download, redistribution, or access to another family's records or private student notes and whiteboards. No public posting, unrelated advertising, sale, or unrestricted model training is authorized by this consent.

Each lesson's transcript and Smart Notes become eligible for deletion one year after that lesson, regardless of continued enrolment (the full retention schedule appears in the Privacy Policy), and lesson materials are kept for 1 year after the last lesson. Preservation exceptions are limited to what is necessary for a legal requirement, an active dispute or legal hold, or an accounting or safety need, and are reviewed at least every 12 months. Children's information is deleted earlier when its purpose is achieved or a parent requests deletion, except where a preservation exception applies. Other records are kept only as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes, the student's service relationship, and applicable legal obligations or claims. Records no longer needed are securely deleted, subject to lawful preservation requirements. Parents and participants may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent at official@alton.education. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not make prior authorized processing unlawful. ALTON stops affected processing as required and resolves future lessons and unused paid credits under the applicable agreement without imposing a penalty for exercising statutory rights. Free learning access is not conditioned on agreeing to lesson recording.

AI notes and transcripts may contain omissions, speaker errors, or inaccuracies. Corrections may be requested. AI output alone does not conclusively determine attendance, wages, fees, refunds, or disputes. Material changes receive updated notice and renewed consent when required.
