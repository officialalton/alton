## Document separation

The English California employment agreement and outside-US teacher services agreement contain operative contract terms and ordinary execution schedules. ## Classification

The California form is a part-time nonexempt employment agreement. The non-US form is conditional on a legally permissible independent service arrangement in the actual work country. Do not select a contractor form solely from nationality, tax form, account role, or a contract title. Teaching is central to the Company's business; actual classification requires particular care. A foreign teacher working from California cannot simply receive the overseas form.

## Preserved and corrected terms

- Preserve USD $50 per compensable hour for California employees, including required nonlesson work; no unpaid preparation or unauthorized-overtime forfeiture.
- Preserve overseas lesson settlement at USD $50 per 60 recognized minutes. Require express scope and compensation for required nonlesson services.
- Preserve monthly overseas settlement, statement within three business days and payment within ten business days after month end. A five-day discrepancy request is not a forfeiture deadline.
- Remove trial AI processing. Align regular lessons with customer signature and all required participant consent. Include text transcription and authorized student/guardian artifact access. Do not authorize retained audio/video recording by implication.
- Separate mandatory payroll, screening, electronic-record, and privacy notices from the contract where independently required. One contract signature does not eliminate those obligations.
- Protect employee wage discussion, reporting, statutory breaks, expenses, final pay, and California invention exclusions. Avoid a general post-employment noncompete.
- Do not introduce the old unfinalized arbitration proposal. The new overseas text preserves lawful court jurisdiction and mandatory local rights; this dispute clause remains an internal release decision.

## Items to complete before execution

These are genuine execution fields, not review warnings in the contract:

1. Company notice address and authorized representative; verify entity details independently.
2. Employee work location, supervisor, start date, payroll period, paydays, and statutory wage notice. No arbitrary payroll frequency has been selected.
3. Overseas actual work country, classification, local mandatory rights and data transfers.
4. Overseas nonlesson compensation, transfer/FX fee allocation, and termination notice period. These were unresolved in v0.1 and remain commercial decisions; the new agreement does not invent values. Complete Schedule A before signing or assigning work.
5. Prior materials and incorporation licenses in Schedule B.
6. Actual AI processing configuration and participant notices; retention policy and access permissions. Contract language does not establish technical implementation.
