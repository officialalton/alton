Date 2026-10-15. Scope: the published exact assignment `data/ap/stock/exact-assign-ushistory-us1-us2.json` (US1, US2) read against `ush-p1b/p2a/p3a-items.json`. ## 1. What the current exams contain

Every stimulus in US1 and US2 is **ALTON-composed** (40 of 40 assigned items; `real_data` is false on every source; each set_intro starts with "ALTON-composed stimulus" and the attribution says fictional author / illustrative data). Nothing is deleted or relabelled; these stay as preserved, classified material. There are **no images, maps, cartoons, photographs or real statistics** anywhere.

## 2. Official stimulus mix (AP Central exam page, retrieved 2026-10-15)

- MC: 55 questions in sets of 3-4 using "primary and secondary sources, images, graphs, and maps". The page gives **no percentages** per type; any exact split is UNVERIFIED (not stated).
- SAQ1 secondary text; SAQ2 primary text; SAQ3 a primary or secondary **non-text** source. SAQ and LEQ are being updated from the May 2027 exam (page note).
- DBQ: 7 documents, "written, quantitative, or visual". The page states **no minimum number of visual documents** (do not assume one).
- LEQ: prompt only.

Gaps against that description: (a) no image/map/graph stimulus in MC (only fictional tables); (b) SAQ3 is a non-text slot and both of ours are fictional tables, so the non-text real-source requirement is unmet; (c) DBQ has no visual document and its quantitative documents are synthetic; (d) 17 of 33 MC stimuli are secondary texts, which cannot be replaced by real modern scholarship (copyrighted); they stay ALTON-composed and labelled, and the mix is better rebalanced by converting some into non-text sets.

## 4. Prioritized reinforcement targets (staged swaps, no mass regeneration)

Method per swap: fix the real source in the registry first, design the skill and key/distractor rationales from it, run the real-source gates and independent solve, then ship as a **new version** of that item through the existing new-version flow (old version stays as the preserved ALTON-composed record). Four items per stage, verify, then decide the next stage.

1. **Stage A: fictional data replaced by real data (highest honesty risk, direct policy hit).**
   US1 `saq3_1` (fictional Dunmore County table; SAQ3 must be non-text) and US2 `saq3_3` (fictional Plains county table): real photograph/map or real Census/Historical Statistics series. US2 `u719` (Red Scare/immigration restriction, fictional table): the registered Census nativity tables fit directly. US1 `u747` (Great Migration, fictional table): Census Black population by region.
2. **Stage B: DBQ (largest single item, 7 documents each; DONE 2026-10-15 on branch `ush-real-b`, see `docs/ap/ushistory-groundwork.md` section 10 — themes changed for US1 to corporations and regulation).** US2 `dbq_1` (Market Revolution) and US1 `dbq_2` (Labor, 1877-1900): replace whole document sets with 7 real documents including at least one visual (not required by the page, but it removes the synthetic chart/table); sample outline in `data/ap/ush-sources/sample-dbq-outline.json`.
3. **Stage C: primary-text sets about famous real events presented through invented documents.** US2 `u526` (Emancipation Proclamation: real text already in the registry), US2 `u408` (Monroe Doctrine), US1 `u528` (Compromise of 1850/Fugitive Slave Act), US1 `u531` (Civil War economies and draft). Remaining primary-text sets (u104, u207, u312, u745, u850, u957, and SAQ2 `saq2_1`/`saq2_2`) follow in later stages.
4. **Stage D: add non-text sets by converting secondary-text sets.** US2 `u527` (Kansas-Nebraska: Reynolds 1856 map already registered), US2 `u720` (Spanish-American War: period political cartoon), US1 `u616` (Plains Indian wars: map or photograph), US2 `u617` (Jim Crow: photograph). Keep the other secondary-text sets labelled composed.

## 6. Rendering

Image stimuli use the existing `ProblemFigure` path: new figure spec `{type: "image", bucket: "static", path: "ap-sources/<id>/<file>", alt, credit}` (`lib/problem-figures/spec.ts`), served from `public/`, with a mandatory visible credit caption under the image (`StaticFigureImage` in `app/session/[id]/ProblemFigure.tsx`), alt text required, path pattern validated (no traversal). Stimulus kind `image` (`lib/ap-figures/normalize.ts`) feeds it; `renderTypeOf` returns `image:static`. Set text for real sources starts with the marker "Historical source (real document)" (`US_REAL_SOURCE_MARKER`), distinct from "ALTON-composed stimulus". Checked with a local `next dev` page (temporary, removed) using the same component: at 390 px the images scale to 343 px wide with no page overflow, at 1280 px they are capped at 736 px; credit captions visible. Screenshots: `docs/ap/ush-real-render/`. Not tested through a full exam session in an isolated Supabase stack (nothing is loaded into any DB).

## 7. Unverified / open

- Exact CED percentages of stimulus types (not published on the AP Central page).
- Real-source items are not loaded anywhere; the new-version flow for the staged swaps was only designed, not run.
- Copyright of Wikimedia-hosted or non-US-government maps was not needed; only LoC/NARA/Census/Avalon/Gutenberg items were used. The Avalon and NARA pages carry no explicit rights banner that was captured; reuse rests on the document's own status (pre-1929 or US government work).
- The Jungle is a novel: items treat it as a muckraking literary source, not a factual record of a plant.
- The 1920-1960 table question treats foreign-born stock, not arrivals; the stem says "in part" because mortality also contributes.
- The Census table values were copied from the PDF text layer of Working Paper 81 and cross-checked only against the paper itself (Table 1 and Table 2 agree on the foreign-born totals; the 1930 row sums to its reported total), not against the original census volumes.
